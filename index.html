Explicitly Flagged Missing Info & Verification Checklist

To keep inputs minimal, this calculator does not guess personal variables not entered above. Before signing an Option to Purchase (OTP), explicitly verify these 4 statutory checkpoints:

1. Age-65 MAS LTV Cap

If (Borrower Age + Loan Tenure) > 65, MAS drops max bank LTV from 75% to 55% (requiring 10% min cash down payment).

2. Variable Income 30% Haircut

Bonuses, commissions, self-employed, or rental income are only recognized at 70% by banks for MAS TDSR calculation.

3. HDB 5-Year MOP & Resale Levy

Confirm your HDB has fulfilled its 5-year Minimum Occupation Period (MOP). Upgrading to a private Condo incurs $0 HDB Resale Levy.

4. CPF Basic Retirement Sum (BRS)

If you are aged 55 and above, CPF refunds go toward your Retirement Account (RA) up to your BRS before excess OA can be used for housing.
